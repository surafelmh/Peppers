Why I Created this Website

I decided to create this website because spices have always been a point of contention within my family. Growing up in an Ethiopian household, spicy food runs rampant within our daily meals. Nearly every meal we would eat would have some spicy aspect to it, with everyone's spice tolerance ranging from none to able to eat any spice. Thinking of this spiked my interest, leading me to want to read up about how spicy peppers occur naturally within nature and how they function.

My Motivation

My main source of motivation towards gaining a deeper understanding of spices is due to my inability to tolerate what my family considers to be small amounts. I was consistently the one to complain first (if not, the only one) about the food being too spicy, while everyone else would chalk it up as me behaving as the youngest expectedly does. I would consistently find myself with various thoughts, ranging from "how are they able to tolerate this?" to eventually "how do these plants even grow naturally?". This random spark of motivation to research this topic struck me, and I realized I could help readers gain this level of understanding with me.

About this Project

I so far have found working on this website to be very informative, despite its simplicity. I never had CSS experience prior to this, and only some HTML practice. I am enthusiastic to continue working on websites similar to this, although I do see myself pursuing a separate topic that I recently brainstormed on. For the time being though, I am enjoying learning more frontend programming, and I believe it will help me improve as a backend developer, which I have always gravitated to more.
